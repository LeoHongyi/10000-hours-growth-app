## Theme

- Concept: Gentle Progression, a warm growth garden instead of a rigid productivity tracker.
- Base background: #fff9f0
- Surface low: #f9f3ea
- Surface: #ffffff / #fff9f0
- Primary: #8a5024
- Primary container: #ffb37e
- Secondary container: #fdd73b
- Tertiary / growth: #4a6545 and #aecca5
- Text: #1d1b16, softened secondary text #52443b

## Typography

- Headlines: Quicksand, 24-32px, 600-700 weight.
- Body and labels: Plus Jakarta Sans, 13-18px, 400-700 weight.
- Labels use uppercase tracking for small metadata.

## Components

- Cards: large rounded surfaces, soft sun-tinted shadows.
- Buttons: pill-shaped, tactile scale interaction.
- Inputs: recessed cream wells with inner shadow.
- Progress: pebble tracks rather than straight technical bars.
- Milestones: sticker-like badges with white border and warm shadow.
- Navigation: bottom icon tabs with yellow active state.
